## 新增实体类

### PPPUser.java (认证计费用户, 30字段)
关键字段: id, username, passwd, enabled, ppptype, bind_ifname, share, upload, download, ip_addr, expires, duration, cardid, phone, name, packages
内含常量: EnabledType(YES/NO), PPPType(ANY/OVPN/PPPOE)

### PPPOnline.java (认证在线用户)
关键字段: id, username, ppptype, pppdev, interface, ip_addr, mac, session, auth_time, upload, download, expires

### PPPPackage.java (计费套餐)
字段: id, packname, packtime, price, up_speed, down_speed, comment

### OpenVPNServer.java (OpenVPN服务端配置)
字段: auth, comp_lzo, dev_type, topology, method, enabled, port, subnet, mask, push_route, proto, key, status(0=关闭,1=开启)
注: toString 中排除 key 字段(安全考虑)
